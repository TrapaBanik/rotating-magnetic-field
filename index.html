Interactive Rotating Magnetic Field (RMF)

Balanced three-phase stator currents create a constant-magnitude magnetic field that rotates in space. Move the slider or press Play to visualize the magnetic-field vectors.

Resultant RMF

Idealized two-pole motor. The three phase axes are separated by 120 electrical degrees. Negative current reverses the corresponding field vector. The resultant vector has magnitude 1.5 times each phase's peak field.

Equations: iA = Im cos θ; iB = Im cos(θ − 120°); iC = Im cos(θ − 240°). Synchronous speed: Ns = 120f/P.
